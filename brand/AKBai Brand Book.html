01 / Brand Story
Ang pangalan ay may kahulugan.
AKBai comes from a Filipino word with deep meaning. "Akbay" means to put an arm around someone's shoulder, to walk beside them, to accompany. Combined with "AI," it represents something powerful: not a tool that replaces you, but a partner that walks with you every step of your business journey.
Hindi lang software. Kaakbay.
In a world of cold, corporate AI solutions, we chose a name that speaks to the heart. AKBai exists to lighten your load, to be there when you need support, and to help you succeed — not by replacing your judgment, but by amplifying your ability to lead.
Vision
To be the most trusted AI partner for Filipino entrepreneurs, enabling them to compete globally while staying true to their values.
Mission
To provide intelligent, human-centered business solutions that understand Filipino business culture and empower entrepreneurs to scale with confidence.

02 / Logo
The Mark — Meet Kai
Logo Anatomy
The AKBai logo mark is a yin-yang inspired circular form featuring Kai's smiling face — the AI persona — at its center. The light variant on cream is the primary logo used across all key brand touchpoints.
Three Components
1
Circular Mark
A yin-yang inspired form with Kai's integrated smiling face. Rendered in Warm Honey gradient (#F59E0B to #D97706) on light backgrounds, full-color on dark, or white + light grey on honey/gradient backgrounds.
2
Kai's Face
Two curved happy eyes and a gentle smile arc at the center. This is the primary visual element — it transforms the logo from an abstract shape into a character users connect with emotionally.
3
Wordmark
"AKB" in on-surface color (#1c1c18 light / #e6e2db dark) + "ai" in primary-container (#f59e0b). Plus Jakarta Sans, ExtraBold 800.
Minimum Size & Clear Space
Minimum Size
24px for favicon, 48px for app icon, 64px for UI avatar
Clear Space
Half the mark's radius on all sides

02 / Logo
Logo Rules
Do
  • Use on approved backgrounds (dark navy, cream, white, honey, or brand gradient)
  • Maintain minimum 24px size for legibility
  • Keep clear space of half the mark's radius on all sides
  • Kai's face must always be visible — never crop or obscure it
Don't
  • Stretch, squeeze, or distort the logo
  • Remove Kai's face from the mark for any application
  • Change the colors — use official hex values only
  • Place on clashing or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Rotate the wordmark (mark orientation is fixed)

03 / Color
Color Balance & Usage
Color Balance
Ink 60%
Honey 25%
Teal 10%
5%
Ink dominates to create a grounded, premium feel. Honey draws the eye to actions and highlights. Teal is used sparingly for success states and secondary accents. Neutrals fill the gaps.
Contrast Combinations
Do
  • Honey on Ink — High contrast, primary CTA
  • White on Card — Maximum contrast for body text
  • White on Ink — Clean dark-mode text
Don't
  • Muted on Cream — Poor contrast ratio
  • Teal on Cream — Insufficient contrast
  • White on Honey — Fails WCAG AA
Usage by Role
Primary Text Ink on light, White on dark
CTA / Accent Warm Honey (#F59E0B)
Success States Teal (#20C9A0)
Backgrounds Ink (dark), Warm Cream (light)
Secondary Text Muted (#8A9BB5)

Typographic Rules
  • Headings: Line height 1.2, slight negative letter-spacing for warmth
  • Body text: Line height 1.6 to 1.8 for comfortable reading
  • Labels: Medium 500 weight, uppercase with letter-spacing for UI elements
  • Conversational Filipino headlines: Filipino syntactic frame (VSO, second-position enclitics) — not translated English, not Taglish SVO
  • Numbers: Tabular figures for financial data alignment
  • Logo wordmark: Always ExtraBold 800, never retype — use the logo asset

09 / Email
Welcome Sequence
Our 4-email welcome sequence nurtures new signups from curiosity to conversion. Each email builds on the last, deepening the relationship.
Day Purpose Focus
Day 0 Welcome + Free Trial Warm welcome, set expectations, provide immediate value. Make them feel seen.
Day 2 Icebreaker + Walkthrough Show them around. Reduce friction. Help them get their first win quickly.
Day 7 Value + Progress Stats Show them what they've accomplished. Reinforce the value of continuing.
Day 13 Convert + Early-Bird Offer Gentle nudge. Special pricing. Create urgency without pressure.

09 / Email
Email Voice
Dominant Archetype
Caregiver — Personal, one-to-one conversations.
Subject Line Formulas
Question-Based
"Alam mo ba kung magkano ang kita mo this month?"
Benefit-Driven
"Save 3 hours a day on invoicing"
Curiosity
"Maria saved P15k last month. Here's how."
Email Voice Rules
Do
  • Write like you're texting a friend who asked for advice
  • Use their name. Reference their business type.
  • Keep paragraphs short — 2-3 sentences max
Don't
  • Use "Dear Valued Customer" or corporate greetings
  • Write walls of text — break it up
  • Hard-sell or use pressure tactics

10 / Digital
Design Philosophy
Warm, Not Tech.
Warm
  • Honey accents that draw the eye naturally
  • Cream backgrounds that feel inviting
  • Rounded corners that soften every edge
  • Conversational Filipino copy that speaks their language
Not
  • Gray palettes that feel cold and corporate
  • Harsh lines and sharp edges
  • Stock photos of generic office workers
  • English-only interfaces
Five UI Principles
01
Warm Backgrounds
Cream, soft whites. Never cold gray.
02
Rounded Corners
8-12px border-radius on all elements.
03
Amber Glow
Subtle honey-tinted shadows.
04
Conversational Filipino Copy
UI labels in natural conversational Filipino.
05
Real Filipino Imagery
Actual Filipino faces, not stock.

10 / Digital
UI Guidelines
Component Styling
Buttons
Rounded (8px radius), honey fill for primary, ghost for secondary. 44px minimum touch target for mobile.
Cards
12px radius, warm-card background (#FFF8ED), ambient shadow instead of borders. Tonal layering over lines.
Inputs
8px radius, subtle cream background, honey focus ring. Placeholder text in Muted (#8A9BB5).
Navigation
Bottom nav for mobile. Honey active indicator. Icons with conversational Filipino labels where space permits.
Shadows & Elevation
Use tonal layering and ambient shadows instead of borders. The No-Line Rule creates a softer, warmer visual hierarchy.
