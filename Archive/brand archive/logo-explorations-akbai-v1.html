AKBai — 6 Directions

Six interpretations of the akbay gesture — semi-abstract and fully abstract — each paired with the "AKBai" wordmark in camelCase. Color is still open; three moods are explored at the bottom. Pick a direction (or parts of several) to take forward.

The Arc + Dot

The arm of the akbay gesture distilled to its two essential elements: the arc (the arm itself) and the dot (the contact point where the arm meets the shoulder). The Embrace

A large open arc — almost a full circle but with a deliberate gap — wraps protectively around a smaller inner circle. The outer arc is the AI partner's presence, enveloping but never fully enclosing (the gap signals openness, not control). The inner circle is the user's business. The dot at the center represents clarity — the Maria Moment. Three layers of meaning in a simple mark. More complex than Dir 01, which could limit scalability below 32px.

The Reach — Fully abstract

AKBai
Your AI business partner
Direction 04
The Reach
Fully abstractMotion

One single confident curve — the motion of the akbay gesture, not the form. It starts from lower-left (origin, the AI reaching out), arcs upward and over (the reach), and lands at a dot on the right (the moment of contact). Most dynamic of the six. The asymmetry gives it energy and direction — always moving forward. Fully abstract means it doesn't read as "a person" — it reads as movement, warmth, and intent.

Dual Arcs
Fully abstractPartnership

Two mirrored arcs rising from a shared baseline and meeting at a single point at the top. Coral from the left (the AI), teal from the right (the user) — converging at the peak. The meeting point (white dot) represents the moment of true partnership. No hierarchy — both arcs are identical in weight. The shared baseline grounds them. The only direction that uses two colors in the mark itself, which requires care in single-color contexts but creates the richest narrative.

Wordmark Arc — Typographic

AKBai
Your AI business partner
Direction 06
Wordmark Arc
TypographicGesture

No separate mark — the wordmark IS the logo. "AKB" in white weight-800, "ai" in the coral gradient (visually flagging the AI component). A thin arc accent floats above the full wordmark, as if the word itself is making the akbay gesture. The icon version distills this into hand-drawn "AK" letterforms with the arc. Works beautifully large (landing page, signage) but requires the arc to be legible, which limits minimum size. The "ai" gradient is a subtle but meaningful differentiator.

Color Exploration — Direction 01 applied to 3 moods

Feeling the Right Temperature

AKBai

Coral → Mango

Warm, energetic, Filipino. Breaks from the blue-green of PH fintech. Sunset palette — inviting, human. Best for Maria the baker. Established direction from research.

AKBai

Teal → Blue

Trust, clarity, financial confidence. Closer to fintech norms — could feel safer to some users when handling money data. Cooler, more "tech." Best for agency owner persona.

AKBai

Violet → Indigo

Premium, AI-forward, slightly mysterious. Strong differentiation from all PH MSME competitors. Signals the AI component most explicitly. Could feel less warm for the baker persona — but very strong for premium positioning.
