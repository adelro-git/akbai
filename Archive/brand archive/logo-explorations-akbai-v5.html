Four New Concept Directions

Stepping beyond the arc system into four entirely new metaphor territories — figurative warmth, structural authority, conversational tech, and the Filipino moment of clarity. Each built from a distinct visual logic.

Concept 1 The Hugging Pill — Supportive Companion
AKBai
Concept 1 · Warm · Relational

The Hugging Pill

A tall navy entity (AKBai) extends an arm over the shoulder of a shorter gold form (the MSME owner). Warmth encoded in geometry — two bodies, one gesture. Reads as supportive companion at a glance.

Concept 2 The Protective A — The Guardian
AKBai
Concept 2 · Structural · Authoritative

The Protective A

A bold A-form where the right leg doesn't stop at the apex — it continues into a canopy arc that loops over the left side, forming a shelter. A yellow spark of clarity burns inside the protected space.

Concept 3 The Chat-Sync Loop — Integrated Partner
AKBai
Concept 3 · Tech · Conversational

The Chat-Sync Loop

Two speech bubbles — AKBai in blue and the business owner in amber — overlap in a teal synergy zone. The dot at the intersection marks the moment of shared understanding. Immediately reads as AI-powered conversation.

Concept 4 The Maria Moment Spark — Clarity & Insight
AKBai
Concept 4 · Human · Luminous

The Maria Moment Spark

A single continuous line: a grounded shoulder, then an arm rising in a smooth curve toward a golden 4-pointed star. The "Maria Moment" — when the Filipino business owner sees the answer. Clarity as light.

Designer Notes
Trademark Strength

C1 (Pill + Arm) and C4 (Shoulder Spark) are the most distinctive — unusual silhouettes with no close matches in global AI or PH tech. C2 (Protective A) is readable as an A-form but the canopy extension makes it defensible. C3 (Chat Bubbles) occupies a crowded space.

Brand Fit

C1 best captures the "Akbay" (to put an arm around) etymology. C4 best captures the Filipino human moment. C2 carries authority for B2B trust. C3 speaks most directly to the AI-chat product layer. All four are meaningfully different from the arc directions in v3 and v4.
