Dialing the Gold

Six tones within the gold-amber family — from bright sunlight yellow through classic gold, warm honey, deep amber, cognac, and a flat single-color version. D4-E at 20° throughout.

1 Sunlight
AKBai
Tone 1 · Bright · Optimistic

Sunlight

The brightest end of the family — lemon-gold that reads joyful and high-energy. Light on dark backgrounds it almost glows. Youthful and expressive, but can feel less premium than deeper golds.

#FDE047 → #FBBF24
2 Classic Gold Current
AKBai
Tone 2 · Balanced · Warm

Classic Gold

The locked-in palette. A true medium gold — not too yellow, not too orange. Confident, warm, and distinct in the AI space. Reads as prosperity without being flashy.

#FFB400 → #F59E0B
3 Warm Honey
AKBai
Tone 3 · Rich · Organic

Warm Honey

A step deeper — amber-gold that reads warm and grounded. Less "finance" and more "organic craft." Closer to the colour of raw honey. Still bright enough to pop on dark backgrounds.

#F59E0B → #D97706
4 Deep Amber
AKBai
Tone 4 · Deep · Premium

Deep Amber

The darkest readable gold — rich, weighty, premium. Closer to aged whiskey or kalamay. Very different mood from Sunlight. Strong for upmarket positioning but loses some of the warmth and energy of the lighter tones.

#D97706 → #B45309
5 Cognac
AKBai
Tone 5 · Bold · Energetic

Cognac

Amber pushed into orange-red territory. The most energetic of the family — high contrast, fiery, bold. Edges toward the Coral-Mango palette. Distinctly different personality from classic gold; more aggressive than warm.

#F97316 → #C2410C
6 Solid Gold — no gradient
AKBai
Tone 6 · Pure · Iconic

Solid Gold

The Classic Gold colour (#FFB400) applied flat — no gradient. Surprisingly strong and confident. Without the gradient movement the mark reads cleaner and more iconic. Also the most versatile for print and embossing.

#FFB400 — flat
