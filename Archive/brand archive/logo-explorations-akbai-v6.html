Five More D4 Directions

Pushing D4 Original across five untested dimensions — angle, depth, echo direction, form replacement, and vertical stagger. D4 Original included in the comparison strip for reference.

D4-E The Lean
AKBai
D4-E · Dynamic · Forward

The Lean

D4 Original rotated 20° clockwise. The entire mark tilts forward — same weight, same two-arc relationship, but the energy shifts from a static gateway to something in motion. Tests whether D4 needs to stay vertical.

D4-F Triple Depth
AKBai
D4-F · Layered · Spatial

Triple Depth

Three arcs all opening in the same direction, diminishing in size and opacity. Reads like a signal radiating outward — or depth receding into space. The third arc disappears into the background.

D4-G Flip Echo
AKBai
D4-G · Momentum · Parallel

Flip Echo

The echo arc is flipped to open in the same direction as the main arc — two "((" moving together. The opposing relationship in D4 Original becomes a parallel one. Reads as momentum, velocity, or a doubled signal.

D4-I Arc + Dot
AKBai
D4-I · Focused · Intentional

Arc + Dot

The echo arc replaced by a single weighted dot in the space the arc opens toward. The arc reaches; the dot is what it's reaching for. Cleaner hierarchy, stronger intentionality — the arc and its focal point.

D4-M The Stagger
AKBai
D4-M · Diagonal · Rhythmic

The Stagger

Same arcs, different vertical anchors — main arc rides high, echo arc drops low. The mark has a diagonal rhythm reading top-left to bottom-right. The two forms feel like they're passing each other, mid-conversation.
