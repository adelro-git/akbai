Brand: AKBai (pronounced "Ak-bay") — from the Filipino word "Akbay" (to put an arm around someone's shoulder, to support) + AI. Tagline: "Kaakbay mo sa negosyo." (Your partner in business.) Vision: Be the most trusted AI partner for Filipino entrepreneurs. Mission: Empower Filipino MSMEs with an intelligent operations partner that removes the friction of running a business — so entrepreneurs can focus on growth. Audience: Filipino micro and small business owners (sari-sari stores, food businesses, online sellers, service providers, retail shops). Ages 22–45. Mix of Tagalog and English (Taglish). Not necessarily tech-savvy. Earning ₱30,000–₱350,000/month. Personality: Warm. Approachable. Confident but not corporate. Like a trusted kaibigan (friend) who happens to be brilliant at business. Think: a helpful ate/kuya who has an MBA. Never condescending. Speaks Taglish naturally. Archetypes: Sage + Caregiver hybrid. Smart, grounded, and deeply caring about the user's success.
