Logo Explorations — Round 1

Project KA — 8 Directions

Broad exploration across different metaphors: chat bubbles, shields, hands, sunrise, letters, and abstract marks. Using "Kaya" as the working name for most explorations (strong cultural fit from research). Pick the direction that resonates — we'll iterate deep on the winner.

kaya
Your AI Business Partner
Direction 1 — Chat Bubble

The Conversation Mark

Chat bubble with "K" initial. Immediately communicates the conversational interface. The rounded bubble feels warm and approachable. Tail points left (user-initiated). Works as app icon (bubble alone).

Katuwang mo sa hustle
Direction 2 — Sunrise

The Morning Briefing

A rising sun over a horizon — directly references the "morning briefing" feature and the feeling of a new day, new opportunity. Aspirational without being corporate. The rays suggest the five pillars radiating outward. Filipino sunrise warmth.

kaya
Trusted AI Partner
Direction 3 — Shield

The Trust Mark

Shield + checkmark. Addresses "Tax Terror" and financial data trust head-on. The shield says "we protect your data." The check says "verified, confirmed." Human-in-the-loop concept visualized. May feel too fintech/corporate — needs warmth balance.

kaya
Direction 4 — Lettermark

The App Icon K

Stylized "K" in a rounded square — native app icon shape. The lower diagonal arm uses the coral gradient, creating a dynamic forward motion. Clean, scalable, works at any size. The rounded square is the universal "app" shape — signals mobile-first instantly.

alalay
Support sa bawat hustle
Direction 5 — Open Hands

The Alalay Mark

Two curved lines cradle a circle — representing support holding up your business. "Alalay" means to support/assist physically. The open form suggests accessibility and warmth. The small circle is your business; the curves are your AI partner lifting it up.

lakad
Let's get things done
Direction 6 — Chat + Arrow

The Action Mark

Speech bubble with a forward arrow inside. Chat interface + forward motion. "Lakad" = to go, to process. Says: "tell me what you need, and we'll move." Action-oriented, not passive. The arrow doubles as a "send" icon — familiar to DM users.

kaya
Kaya mo 'to
Direction 7 — Calligraphic Ka

The Connected Letterform

"Ka" as connected letterforms — the K and a flow into each other. The connection point (dot) symbolizes the partnership between user and AI. Typographic approach — elegant, minimal, distinctive. Works well at large sizes; needs simplification at small.

gabay
Gabay mo sa negosyo
Direction 8 — Pulse

The Business Pulse

Circle with a pulse/heartbeat line through it. Your business has a heartbeat — and Gabay monitors it. The pulse represents proactive monitoring: cash flow, deadlines, inventory levels. "We're always watching your business health." Medical metaphor = trust and care.
