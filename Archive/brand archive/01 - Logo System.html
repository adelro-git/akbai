09 — Usage Rules
Do & Don't
✓ Do
Use the primary lockup on dark backgrounds as the default.
Maintain clear space equal to the "A" glyph width on all sides.
Use the reversed (all-white) version on gradient or photographic backgrounds.
Use mono versions for single-colour print, emboss, or engraving.
Scale the icon alone for app icons, favicons, and small placements.
✗ Don't
Don't place the Warm Honey version on coloured backgrounds — use reversed or mono.
Don't rotate the mark beyond its built-in 20° lean.
Don't stretch, skew, or change the proportion of the mark to wordmark.
Don't use the full lockup below 140px wide — use icon only.
Don't add drop shadows, outlines, or effects to the mark.
