Refined Directions

Heavy geometric forms. One or two elements only. No literal body references. The gesture is implied through mass, curvature, and negative space — not depicted.

01 — The Heavy Arc
AKBai

The Heavy Arc

Bold stroke · 1 element

One single form with real mass. The thick C-arc has enough weight to feel like a shape, not a line. The opening on the right creates natural tension — it suggests reach and enclosure simultaneously without depicting either. Scales cleanly because there's nothing to lose at small sizes.

02 — Negative Space
AKBai
Your AI business partner

Negative Space

Filled form · Inverted · 1 element

The same C-shape as D1 but inverted — the gradient fills the square and the gesture is carved out of it as dark negative space. The mark is the absence, not the presence. More energetic and modern. The gradient square also works as a plain app icon slab when the C reads at small sizes.

03 — The Crescent
AKBai
Your AI business partner

The Crescent

Filled shape · Solid mass · 1 element

A solid filled crescent — no stroke, no lines. Pure shape. The gradient runs through the form itself. This is heavier and more confident than a stroked arc. The crescent suggests embrace and enclosure while reading as a distinct, ownable symbol. Works as a standalone icon without any wordmark context.

04 — The Open Gate
AKBai
Your AI business partner

The Open Gate

2 arcs · Layered weight · Shadow form

Two arcs of different weights facing the same direction — the bold coral arc is the AI presence, the ghost arc behind it is the user. The slight offset and opacity difference creates a layered depth without complexity. The two-form relationship is felt, not seen. Most abstract of the six.

05 — The Convergence
AKBai
Your AI business partner

The Convergence

2 strokes + dot · Momentum

Two heavy curves rise from the base and meet at a single dot — the moment of contact. The coral arc is dominant and visible; the white arc is a ghost presence behind it. The dot at the apex is where the partnership is made real. The Maria Moment visualised as geometry: two things converging toward clarity.

06 — The Sweep
AKBai
Your AI business partner

The Sweep

Filled path · Asymmetric · Motion

A single filled asymmetric form — wide at the origin, narrowing as it arcs over. The shape captures the sweeping motion of the akbay gesture as a body of mass rather than a line. No stroke, no outlines — pure filled gradient form. The most distinctly ownable of the six: nothing in Philippine tech looks like this.
