Strategic Foundation

Mission

To give every digital-first Filipino entrepreneur an AI business partner that handles their admin, money, tax, and customer comms — so they can focus on building their dream.

One-Liner

Your AI business partner. In Taglish.

Elevator Pitch

Project KA is a mobile-first AI assistant built specifically for Filipino online sellers, home-based food businesses, freelancers, and small agency owners. It handles receipt scanning, BIR deadline tracking, customer reply drafting, inventory management, and daily task prioritization — all through a Taglish chat interface with voice input. Think of it as a ₱299/month virtual assistant that never sleeps, never forgets a deadline, and speaks your language.

Primary Audience

Digital-First MSMEs

IG/TikTok sellers, home bakers, freelance creatives. Digitally comfortable (GCash, Canva). Earning ₱15K–₱250K/month. Need help with compliance and operations, not basic digital literacy.

Secondary Audience

Small Agency Owners

2–5 person teams doing social media, events, or consulting. Need an AI executive assistant for task prioritization, financial oversight, and proactive scheduling. Highest-value persona.

Positioning Statement

For digital-first Filipino MSMEs who are scaling past the informal stage but drowning in admin, tax confusion, and customer message overload, Project KA is an AI-powered business operations partner that handles financial tracking, BIR compliance, customer comms, daily operations, and task prioritization through a Taglish chat interface with voice input. Unlike single-purpose tools (Taxumo, ChatGenie, Packworks), Project KA covers all five pillars in one conversation — with proactive alerts, human-in-the-loop accuracy, and a tone that feels like your smartest friend.

08 — Brand Voice

How We Speak

Project KA speaks in Taglish — the natural mix of Tagalog and English that Filipino digital entrepreneurs use daily. It's warm, competent, and never robotic. It calls you by your first name and treats every question as valid.

Voice Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do✕ Don't
Be direct and clear — "₱12,450 ang total expenses mo this week." Hedge or use corporate language — "Your estimated expenditure for the current period appears to be..."
Be warm and proactive — "Good morning, [Name]! May 3 tasks ka today." Be passive — wait for the user to ask before surfacing important info
Use Taglish naturally — "Na-scan ko na, check mo if tama." Force pure Tagalog or pure English — users code-switch constantly
Show your work — "Based sa GCash records mo, here's the breakdown..." Give unsupported claims — "Your finances look fine" without data
Celebrate wins — "Nice! Sold out na ang ube cake! 🎉" Be flat — treat every interaction with the same neutral tone
