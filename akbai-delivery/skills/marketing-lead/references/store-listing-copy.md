
## Download Badges — Asset Task

### Where to Get Official Badges

**Apple "Download on the App Store" badge:**
- Source: Apple Marketing Resources — https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/
- Requires an Apple Developer account to access
- Download the current badge artwork from the "Download on the App Store" section
- Available in SVG and PNG, English and localized variants
- Localized Filipino variant: use "I-download sa App Store" if available in the localized set; otherwise the English badge is acceptable per Apple guidelines for markets without a localized badge

**Google Play "Get it on Google Play" badge:**
- Source: Google Play Brand Guidelines — https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/
- Download the badge directly from the badge generator at the link above
- Available in multiple languages including Filipino
- The badge generator outputs PNG and SVG

### Required Sizes

**Apple:**
- Minimum: 135 px tall on light backgrounds (per Apple Human Interface Guidelines)
- Do not scale below the minimum or distort aspect ratio
- Use the SVG source for web and the PNG for mobile app contexts

**Google Play:**
- Minimum: 55 px tall (per Google brand guidelines)
- Preferred: 646 × 250 px (2x retina)
- Do not alter colors, proportions, or add visual effects

### Non-Modification Rule

Both Apple and Google require that official badge artwork be used **unmodified**. This means:

- No color changes, filter effects, or tonal overlays (even to match AKBai's honey palette)
- No rounding of corners beyond what is in the original artwork
- No additional text added to or around the badge beyond what guidelines allow
- No scaling below minimum sizes
- The badge must appear on a background that provides sufficient contrast per each platform's contrast requirements

**Violating badge usage guidelines can result in app store policy flags and required asset corrections before launch.**
