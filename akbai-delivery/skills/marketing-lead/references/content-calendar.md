## 1. Weekly Posting Cadence (Phase 0B)

Anton has 10-15 hours per sprint (2 weeks). Content production must be batched and efficient.

| Day | Channel | Content Type | Time | Notes |
|-----|---------|-------------|------|-------|
| Monday | FB group | Value post (BIR tip or tax guide) | 7:00 AM | Highest engagement for educational content |
| Tuesday | TikTok/IG | "Alam Mo Ba?" short-form | 8:00 PM | Evening scroll time |
| Wednesday | Blog | SEO article publish | 10:00 AM | 1 article per week, batch-written on weekends |
| Thursday | FB group | Story post (build-in-public update) | 8:00 PM | "Day X of building AKBai..." |
| Friday | Email | Waitlist nurture or BIR reminder | 9:00 AM | Timed before weekend |
| Saturday | FB group | Engagement post (question/poll) | 9:00 AM | Weekend = higher FB engagement |
| Sunday | Batch prep | Write next week's content | Anytime | 2-3 hours to prep all content for the week |

**Batch creation strategy:** Write 5-7 FB posts + 1 blog article on Sunday. Schedule everything. This means Anton only needs 2-3 hours of focused content time per week, with the rest of the sprint for product work.

## 2. Facebook Value Post Templates

### Template A: BIR Tip Post
```
[HOOK — 1 line, addresses a specific BIR pain]

Alam mo ba na [specific BIR fact or deadline]?

[2-3 short paragraphs explaining what it means and what to do]

[Specific numbers: forms, dates, amounts]

Quick tip: [1 actionable thing the reader can do right now]

Reminder: Ito ay gabay lamang, hindi tax advice. Kumonsulta sa CPA para sa opisyal na payo.

---
[Optional soft CTA: "Save this post — kailangan mo 'to."]
```

**Example:**
```
Alam mo ba na 20 BIR tax deadlines ang kailangan mo i-track every year?

Hindi lang April 15 — bente talaga.

Kung online seller ka or may tindahan, eto yung deadlines na madalas ma-miss:
- 2551Q (Quarterly Percentage Tax) — every Feb 25, May 25, Aug 25, Nov 25
- 1701Q (Quarterly Income Tax) — every May 15, Aug 15, Nov 15
- 0605 (Annual Registration Fee) — January 31

Isa lang ang ma-miss, 25% surcharge + 20% interest agad.

Pro tip: I-calendar mo na lahat ngayon — mas mura ang 30 minutes ng setup kaysa sa penalty.

Reminder: Guide lang po ito — kumonsulta sa CPA para sa official tax advice.

I-save mo 'tong post para may reference ka!
```

## 5. Email Sequence Templates

### Waitlist Nurture Sequence (5 emails over 14 days)

**Email 1 — Welcome (Day 0)**
Subject: "Welcome sa waitlist, [Name]! Eto ang una mong BIR tip."
Body: Thank them. Share 1 genuinely useful BIR tip. Set expectations for what AKBai will do. "I'll send you useful business tips while you wait — no spam, promise."

**Email 2 — Value (Day 3)**
Subject: "Alam mo ba magkano ang penalty pag late ang BIR filing mo?"
Body: BIR penalty breakdown with real numbers. Position AKBai's deadline reminders as the solution.

**Email 3 — Story (Day 7)**
Subject: "Bakit ko ginagawa ang AKBai"
Body: Anton's founder story. Day job at Globe, building at night. Why Filipino MSMEs deserve better tools. Personal, vulnerable, honest.

**Email 4 — Social proof (Day 10)**
Subject: "[X] negosyante na ang nag-sign up"
Body: Waitlist count update. 1-2 testimonials from beta testers if available. Feature preview.

**Email 5 — Urgency (Day 14)**
Subject: "Malapit na ang launch — ready ka na ba?"
Body: Launch timeline update. Early access benefit for waitlist members. Referral CTA: "Mag-refer ng kaibigan — pareho kayong makakapasok agad."

Subject: "[X] days before [Form Name] deadline — ready ka na ba?"
Body: What the form is. Who needs to file. What to prepare. Link to blog article guide. AKBai mention: "AKBai will remind you automatically — join the waitlist."

## 6. BIR Deadline Content Schedule

These are the 20 annual BIR deadlines. Plan content 2 weeks before each deadline for maximum relevance.

| Deadline | Form | Who Files | Content Window |
|----------|------|-----------|----------------|
| Jan 31 | 0605 (Annual Registration) | All registered businesses | Jan 15-30 |
| Feb 25 | 2551Q (Q4 Percentage Tax) | Non-VAT businesses | Feb 10-24 |
| Mar 31 | Annual ITR (1701/1702) | All businesses | Mar 15-30 |
| Apr 15 | 1701Q (Q1 Income Tax) | Self-employed/professionals | Apr 1-14 |
| Apr 25 | 2550Q (Q1 VAT) | VAT-registered | Apr 10-24 |
| May 25 | 2551Q (Q1 Percentage Tax) | Non-VAT businesses | May 10-24 |
| Jul 25 | 2550Q (Q2 VAT) | VAT-registered | Jul 10-24 |
| Aug 15 | 1701Q (Q2 Income Tax) | Self-employed/professionals | Aug 1-14 |
| Aug 25 | 2551Q (Q2 Percentage Tax) | Non-VAT businesses | Aug 10-24 |
| Oct 25 | 2550Q (Q3 VAT) | VAT-registered | Oct 10-24 |
| Nov 15 | 1701Q (Q3 Income Tax) | Self-employed/professionals | Nov 1-14 |
| Nov 25 | 2551Q (Q3 Percentage Tax) | Non-VAT businesses | Nov 10-24 |

**Content strategy around deadlines:**
- T-14 days: Publish SEO blog article ("Paano mag-file ng [form]")
- T-7 days: FB value post (deadline reminder + quick guide)
- T-3 days: TikTok/IG countdown ("3 days na lang!")
- T-1 day: Email blast to waitlist
- T+1 day: FB post ("Na-file mo na ba? Here's what to do kung na-miss mo")

## 7. Micro-Influencer Outreach Scripts (Phase 2)

Target: 5-10 creators who are themselves MSME owners and post about business/hustle content in Taglish. Not traditional influencers — real business owners with 5K-50K followers.

### DM Script — Initial Outreach
```
Hi [Name]! Napansin ko yung content mo about [specific post/topic] — super helpful for fellow negosyante.

I'm Anton, and I'm building AKBai — an AI business partner for Filipino MSMEs. Think of it like having a smart katulong for BIR deadlines, receipt tracking, and daily business updates — lahat in Taglish.

Hindi ko hinihingi na mag-post ka about it — gusto ko lang i-offer ang free Business tier account (₱899/mo value) for you to try. Kung useful siya sa negosyo mo and you naturally want to share it, amazing. Kung hindi, no worries at all.

Would you be open to trying it?
```

### Follow-Up (if interested)
```
Salamat, [Name]! Eto ang setup:

1. I'll give you a Business tier account — full access, no time limit
2. Try it for 2 weeks sa actual negosyo mo
3. If you like it, feel free to share — pero walang obligation
4. I'd love your honest feedback either way — it helps me make it better

Anong email mo para ma-set up kita?
```

### Key Rules for Influencer Program:
- No scripted posts. Ever. Authentic sharing only.
- No payment. Barter only (free Business tier = ₱899/mo value).
- No minimum post requirements. If they don't like it, they don't post. That's data.
- Prioritize creators who are actual MSME owners, not lifestyle influencers.
- Track: did their followers actually sign up? (referral codes)

## 8. FB Group Admin Partnership Scripts

Target: Admins of Negosyante PH, Online Sellers PH, Freelancers PH, and similar groups (50K-500K members each).

### DM Script — Admin Outreach
```
Hi [Name]! I'm Anton — I've been a member of [Group Name] for [X weeks/months] and I've been answering BIR and tax questions in the group.

I'm building AKBai — an AI business partner that helps Filipino MSMEs track expenses, handle BIR deadlines, and get daily business updates, lahat in Taglish. Solo founder, building this after my day job.

I'd love to offer you a permanent free Business tier account (₱899/mo value) — not as a promo deal, but because I think you'd genuinely find it useful for your own business, and your feedback would be invaluable.

No obligation to post about it. Pero if you end up liking it and want to mention it sa group, that would mean the world.

Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call or Messenger chat?
```

### Key Rules:
- Only approach admins AFTER establishing genuine presence in the group (2-4 weeks minimum of value posts).
- One admin endorsement in a 50K+ group = estimated 100+ waitlist signups.
- Never ask them to post. The product should earn the endorsement.
- Permanent free Business tier — not time-limited. This builds genuine long-term advocacy.
