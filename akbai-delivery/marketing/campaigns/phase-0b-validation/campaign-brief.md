## Channels

### 1. Free BIR Tools (M1-M2) -- Primary Conversion Channel
Standalone tools on akbai.vercel.app that deliver immediate value:

- **BIR Deadline Checker** -- Enter business type, see 2026 deadlines with countdown. Highest priority.
- **8% Flat Tax Calculator** -- Compare flat tax vs graduated rates based on income.

Each tool ends with waitlist CTA. These are the #1 driver of signups because they attract people with active BIR pain.

## Content Hooks (Validated from Market Research)

These hooks use real language from Reddit, Facebook, and TikTok. They tested well because they use the exact words MSMEs use to describe their pain.

### Primary Hooks

| Hook | Pain Point | Why It Works |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| "Hindi na nakakatakot ang BIR deadline." | BIR anxiety | "Nakakatakot" is the exact word MSMEs use on TikTok/FB |
| "Alam mo na ba kung kumikita ka?" | Cash flow blindness | Echoes real quote: "hindi ko alam kung kumikita ba o hindi" |
| "₱399/month vs ₱11,800/month sa accountant." | Cost | Real pricing comparison from Reddit |
| "Snap mo lang, tracked na." | Receipt tracking pain | Contrast to FB groups asking "Excel pa rin?" |

### Supporting Hooks

| Hook | Context |
|------|---------|
| "20 BIR deadlines per year. Isa lang ang ma-miss, 25% surcharge agad." | Fear-with-facts |
| "99.5% ng businesses sa Pilipinas, MSME. Deserve nila ng AI partner." | Aspirational |
| "Hindi ka nag-iisa sa negosyo mo." | Brand promise |
| "Si Kai na ang sasagot -- ikaw na lang mag-send." | DM overload relief |
| "Goodbye Excel. Goodbye notebook. Hello Kai." | Manual → automatic |
