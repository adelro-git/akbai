# SEO Keyword Research -- Phase 0B
> Initial keyword targets validated from market sentiment research
> Last updated: 2026-04-03

---

## Keyword Strategy

**Approach:** Target Taglish and English long-tail keywords that Filipino MSMEs actually search for. The Philippine MSME SEO space is severely underserved -- most BIR content is either government legalese or CPA firm advertising. Taglish content that genuinely helps ranks fast.

**Content language:** Taglish -- Tagalog carries the conversational flow, English for technical/business terms. This matches how our audience actually searches and reads.

## SEO Technical Requirements

### On-Page
- Title tag: Include primary keyword, Taglish, under 60 characters
- Meta description: Include keyword + value proposition, under 155 characters
- H1: One per page, keyword-rich, Taglish
- H2/H3: Use related keywords and questions
- Internal links: Link to BIR Deadline Checker tool + related articles
- BIR disclaimer at bottom of every tax article

### Schema Markup (JSON-LD)
Every article should include:
- `Article` schema (headline, author, datePublished, dateModified)
- `FAQPage` schema (3-5 questions per article -- this powers AEO)
- `BreadcrumbList` schema

See `seo/schema-templates.md` for JSON-LD templates.

### Technical SEO
- Sitemap.xml at akbai.vercel.app/sitemap.xml
- robots.txt allowing all crawling
- Canonical URLs on all pages
- OG tags + Twitter Card tags on every page
- Page speed: target <3s LCP (Vercel handles this well)
