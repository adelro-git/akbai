## Target Personas (GTM Priority Order)

Persona prioritization for marketing -- who we reach first, and why.

### 1. Ana (Freelancer) -- Priority: HIGHEST
- **Why first:** Highest digital literacy among all personas. Already online (LinkedIn, Reddit, Facebook freelancer groups). Most likely to discover AKBai through search or social media.
- **Primary pain:** BIR anxiety -- 8% flat tax confusion, quarterly 1701Q filing, fear of penalties. "Nakakatakot" is the exact word they use.
- **Acquisition channel:** SEO (searches "8% flat tax freelancer Philippines"), Reddit (r/buhaydigital, r/phinvest), Facebook freelancer groups.
- **Hook:** "Hindi na nakakatakot ang BIR deadline."
- **Content that converts:** Step-by-step BIR guides, 8% flat tax explainers, freelancer tax calculator tool.

### 2. Jose (Online Seller) -- Priority: HIGH
- **Why second:** Highest revenue among personas, already using digital platforms (Shopee, Lazada, GCash). Furious about platform fees and tax on online sales.
- **Primary pain:** GCash income tracking, VAT confusion for online sellers, platform fee impact on margins.
- **Acquisition channel:** Facebook seller groups (Online Sellers PH, Shopee Sellers PH), TikTok business content.
- **Hook:** "₱399/month vs ₱11,800/month sa accountant."
- **Content that converts:** GCash income tax guide, online selling tax explainer, margin calculator tool.

### 3. Maria (Food Business) -- Priority: MEDIUM
- **Why third:** Strongest community networks -- word-of-mouth is her primary discovery channel. Slightly lower digital literacy but highly active on Facebook.
- **Primary pain:** Receipt chaos, cash flow blindness ("hindi ko alam kung kumikita ba o hindi"), BIR compliance for home-based food businesses.
- **Acquisition channel:** Facebook food business groups, referrals from other Marias, community events.
- **Hook:** "Alam mo na ba kung kumikita ka?"
- **Content that converts:** Receipt tracking tips, food business costing guides, BIR registration guide for home bakers.

### 4. Andoy (Sari-Sari) -- Priority: DEFERRED (Phase 3+)
- **Why deferred:** Lowest digital literacy, least likely to discover a PWA through search or social media. Needs direct community outreach (barangay events, DTI partnerships) that doesn't scale in Phase 0B.
- **Future play:** DTI/Negosyo Center partnerships, QR flyer distribution, local community events.

## Top 3 Actions

### Action 1: Free BIR Tools (M1-M2) -- "The Tool IS the Marketing"
Build standalone free tools that deliver immediate value and capture waitlist emails:

- **BIR Deadline Checker** -- Enter your business type, see your 2026 deadlines with countdown timers. This is the #1 priority tool.
- **8% Flat Tax Calculator** -- "Should I choose 8% flat tax or graduated rates?" Input income, see the comparison.
- **Receipt Cost Calculator** -- "How much are you spending on manual bookkeeping?" Compare manual vs AKBai cost.

Each tool ends with: "Gusto mo ng automatic reminders? Join the waitlist."

### Action 2: SEO + AEO Foundation (M2-M4)
Publish 5-6 Taglish articles targeting validated keywords:

| Keyword | Search Intent | Article Type |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| "BIR deadline 2026 Philippines" | Informational | Comprehensive deadline calendar |
| "paano mag-file ng 1701Q" | How-to | Step-by-step guide |
| "8% flat tax freelancer Philippines" | Informational | Comparison guide |
| "GCash income tax Philippines" | Informational | Online seller tax guide |
| "online selling tax Philippines" | Informational | Complete tax guide for sellers |
| "small business expense tracker Philippines" | Commercial | Comparison + AKBai mention |

Each article includes FAQ schema (for AEO), BIR disclaimer, and a single soft CTA to the waitlist.

### Action 3: Content Repurposing Engine (M3)
Set up a repeatable pipeline:
```
1 Blog Article (1,200-1,800 words)
  → 3 Facebook value posts (extracted key points)
  → 1 TikTok/IG script (15-30 sec "Alam Mo Ba?")
  → 1 Email to waitlist (key insight + link to full article)
  → 1 Carousel/infographic (key data points)
```
This lets Anton produce 6+ pieces of content from 1 writing session.

## AKBai-Specific Strategies

### BIR Deadline Viral Loops
BIR deadlines are the highest-anxiety, most time-sensitive content in the MSME space. Strategy:

- **T-14 days:** Publish SEO article ("Paano mag-file ng [form]")
- **T-7 days:** Facebook value post (deadline reminder + quick guide)
- **T-3 days:** TikTok/IG countdown ("3 days na lang!")
- **T-1 day:** Email blast to waitlist
- **T+1 day:** Facebook post ("Na-file mo na ba? Here's what to do kung na-miss mo")

The BIR Deadline Checker tool makes this self-reinforcing: users bookmark it, share it before deadlines, and return every quarter.

### CPA Referral Channel
A CPA on r/BusinessPH is actively researching "a software/digital product to make filing easier." CPAs are natural referral partners:

- AKBai handles the daily tracking; CPA handles the annual filing
- Offer CPAs a referral dashboard or affiliate fee (Phase 1+)
- Position AKBai as "the tool that makes your clients' books ready for you"

### Facebook Group Value Bombing
The primary organic acquisition play. 3 target groups with 1M+ combined members:

1. **Negosyante PH** -- general MSME group
2. **Online Sellers PH** -- Jose persona
3. **Freelancers PH** -- Ana persona

**Playbook:**
- Weeks 1-2: Join groups. Read. Understand the vibe. Answer real BIR questions helpfully. Zero mentions of AKBai.
- Weeks 3-4: Start posting value content (Template A: BIR Tip Posts). Build credibility. Still no product mentions.
- Week 5+: Introduce build-in-public story posts (Template B). "I'm building something for this exact problem." Soft CTA: "Comment BETA if you want early access."

One genuine admin endorsement in a 50K+ group = estimated 100+ waitlist signups.

### DTI/Negosyo Center QR Flyers (Phase 1+)
For Maria and Andoy personas who won't find us online:

- Partner with DTI Negosyo Centers (government MSME support offices)
- QR code flyers: "Libreng BIR deadline checker para sa negosyo mo" → links to free tool
- Low cost (₱500 for 500 flyers), distributed where MSMEs already go for help
- Deferred to Phase 1 when we have a custom domain and polished free tools
