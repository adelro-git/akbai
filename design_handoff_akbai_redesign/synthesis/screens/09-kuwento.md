## 4. Enrichments applied

- `pattern:peak-end-weekly-close` — auto-opens Sunday 8–11 AM Manila; engineered emotional climax; takeaway always lands warm even on negative weeks
- `pattern:privacy-safe-viral-share` — Linggong Kuwento itself is **private** (no in-app share CTA). Viral surfaces (streak card + BIR-cert card) render separately and contain no peso amounts. Anton's Q12 override on hiya around earnings disclosure
- `pattern:hooked-variance-coach-not-casino` — Kai narrative varies in content, not in reliability; three tonal registers rotate; user always gets something supportive
- `pattern:endowed-progress-streaks` — 2×2 KPI grid includes streak count as one of the 4 metrics: "ARAW NA MAY CHECK-IN: X/7"
- `pattern:varying-kai-expression-by-context` — if a KPI card is opened to full detail, Kai avatar shows `celebrating` on record weeks, `happy` on normal weeks, `concerned` only if data context is explicitly negative (e.g., 0/7 tubo days)
- `pattern:reduced-motion-respect` — no decorative animations on Kuwento (no petals, no kai-bob); layout delivers warmth via palette + typography alone
