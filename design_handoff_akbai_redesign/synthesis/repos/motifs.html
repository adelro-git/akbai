CapizPattern

Background pattern referencing capiz windowpane oysters — heritage Filipino architecture motif. Tiled 60×60 squares at 0.18 opacity. Fills the entire backdrop, never the foreground.

Use: Behind hero card · paper-note callouts · weekly story background

opacity 0.18-0.35 (light) · 0.12-0.20 (dark) · pointer-events none

on white (#fdf9f2)
on honey-cream (#fef4dd)
on dark mode (#1a1410)
Source: Phase 1 design-system §6 — capiz is one of two inclusive primary motifs (along with banig). Tile: 60×60 unit, 4-cell quartet with alternating opacity. Reads better on: honey-cream (subtle texture without competing with content). Phase 1 rule: Filipino architecture heritage; subtle, never dominant; survives WCAG contrast checks against text on top.

WovenDivider

Banig-weave dashes in zigzag pattern. Replaces the original sampaguita garland (rejected as "too feminine for some users"). 32-segment SVG, alternating up/down strokes.

Use: Section divider on home (between action grid and weekly story)

opacity 0.55 · color #b06410 · stroke 1.2

on white
on honey-cream
Style: 32 alternating strokes simulating banig (sleeping mat) weave. Phase 1 design-system §6: Banig is the inclusive primary motif — neutral, working-class, unisex. Reviewer note: "chosen over sampaguita garlands because they might be too feminine for some users." Note: Honey-cream version has lower contrast — may need opacity bump to 0.7 on honey background.

All motifs — dark mode preview

AKBai supports dark mode (existing project rule). All decorative motifs need to read correctly on the dark surface (#1a1410) without losing the "warm" feel. Motifs that go invisible or muddy in dark mode get rejected or get a dark-specific variant.
