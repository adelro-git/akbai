The 6 KaXxx.tsx expression variants swap eyes/eyebrows/mouth on this base form. They power Kai's emotional context across the app:
  • KaWaving — onboarding step 1, login
  • KaThinking — wait states, "let me check..."
  • KaHappy — default home, post-action
  • KaCelebrating — milestones, completion
  • KaConcerned — tight cash flow, deadline near
  • KaWorking — OCR processing, syncing

Proposed style rules (becomes canonical if approved)

Brand-icon rules:

  • ViewBox: 60×60 for brand icons; 28×28 for nav icons; 20×20 for sampaguita.
  • Stroke weight: 1.5 for outer shapes, 1.6 for primary, 1.8 for face lines, 1.2 for fine details.
  • Colors: stroke #b06410 or #8a4e0a; gradient stops #fffbf0→#fde8c0 (paper) or #f5b347→#c87b14 (honey). Active state: #f5b347 fill, #8a3d0a stroke.
  • Soft shadow: ellipse below shape at cx=30, cy=54, rx=15-18, ry=2, fill #c89a3e opacity 0.22.
  • Faces: Resibo and Pera have small smiles. Limit faces to 2-3 icons total — too many faces feels infantile.
  • Text in SVG: use <text> only when essential (peso ₱). Avoid text leak to aria-label.
  • aria-hidden: set aria-hidden="true" on all decorative icons. Provide accessible label on the parent button.

When to use a brand icon vs lucide-react:

  • Brand icon: feature tile, page header, paper-note callout, primary CTA decoration.
  • lucide-react: utility (close ✕, chevron, settings ⚙, search 🔍, filter, ellipsis-vertical, ...).
  • Never both for the same role — pick one and commit.
